# 5. Everyday tasks

Here are real, practical things you can do with free AI today. Each one includes a prompt you can copy and paste.

## Write emails and letters

- Thank-you note after a job interview
- Note to your landlord requesting a repair
- Email to a coworker covering your shift

**Prompt:**
> Write a short, polite [thank-you note / request email] to [person]. Keep it under 100 words and use a friendly tone.

## Explain hard topics

- Medical terms from a doctor's visit (for education only — not a diagnosis)
- A bill or letter you do not understand
- A news topic you want to learn about

**Prompt:**
> Explain [topic] in plain language. Use short sentences. Give one everyday example.

## Plan meals and shopping

- Dinner ideas with ingredients you already have
- A grocery list for one week
- Simple recipes that take under 30 minutes

**Prompt:**
> I have [ingredients]. Suggest three simple dinners under 30 minutes. Then make a grocery list for anything I still need.

## Organize paperwork and life admin

- Turn a messy list into a neat to-do list
- Draft a budget outline
- Break a big project into small steps

**Prompt:**
> Here is a messy list of things I need to do: [paste list]. Organize them into a simple to-do list, grouped by category, with the most important items first.

## Help with writing and job searches

- Resume bullet points
- Cover letter drafts
- Practice interview questions

**Prompt:**
> I am applying for a [job title]. Write three short resume bullet points that highlight [skills you have]. Keep each under two lines.

## Healthcare workers (education and admin only)

- Draft shift-handover templates (no patient names)
- Explain general health topics in plain language for patients (education only)
- Summarize a public health article

**Prompt:**
> Create a blank shift-handover template for a nurse. Include sections for patient count, tasks pending, and concerns. Do not include any real patient names or details.

**Important:** This is education and administration only. Never put real patient information into a free AI chat. AI is never a substitute for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

## Books, publishing, and small business (Asdale Dynamics focus)

- Book description or back-cover blurb
- Marketing copy for a product or service
- Simple invoice or bookkeeping notes
- eBay or Amazon listing titles and descriptions

**Prompt:**
> Write a friendly, honest product description for [item]. Mention [condition / key features]. Keep it under 80 words.

## Gaming and fun (FFXIV and chess)

- Chess opening explanations for beginners
- FFXIV quest or class summaries in plain language
- Game strategy tips written simply

**Prompt:**
> Explain [chess opening / FFXIV class] like I am brand new to it. Use short sentences and avoid jargon.

## Try one right now

Pick one task from this list. Open a free AI chat and copy the prompt. Fill in the brackets with your own words. Read the answer. If it is not quite right, ask it to try again.
