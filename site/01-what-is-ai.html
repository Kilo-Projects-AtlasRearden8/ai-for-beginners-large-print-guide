1. What is AI?

AI stands for artificial intelligence. In plain English: AI is a computer program that can read, write, and answer questions in a way that feels like talking to another person.

What AI actually is

Think of AI as a very smart autocomplete. You have probably used autocomplete on your phone — it guesses the next word as you type. AI does the same thing, but with much more practice. It has read huge amounts of text (books, websites, articles) and learned patterns in how people write.

When you type a question, AI guesses the best next words to give you a helpful answer.

What AI is NOT

  • AI is not a person. It has no feelings, no beliefs, and no body.
  • AI is not always right. It can make things up. This is called “hallucinating.”
  • AI does not “know” things the way you do. It predicts words.
  • AI is not conscious. It does not think or feel.

What free AI can help you do

Free AI tools can help you with everyday tasks like:

  • Writing emails or letters
  • Summarizing long articles
  • Planning meals or trips
  • Explaining hard topics in simple words
  • Drafting resumes or cover letters
  • Brainstorming ideas

Where free AI comes from

Several companies offer free AI chat tools you can use in a web browser or phone app. You do not need to install anything, and you do not need to pay to get started. You can also run some AI on your own computer (called “local AI”), but this guide focuses on the free tools you can use right away in your browser.

Things to keep in mind

  1. Never share private information with an AI (more on that in the privacy section).
  2. Double-check important facts. AI can be wrong about dates, names, laws, and medicine.
  3. AI is a tool, not an expert. Use it to get started, then review the results yourself.

Try it

Open a free AI chat in your browser. Type this and press enter:

Hello! Can you tell me a simple joke?

You should get a friendly answer back. That is your first conversation with AI.
