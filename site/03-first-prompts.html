3. Your first prompts

A prompt is just what you type to the AI. The better your prompt, the better the answer. You do not need special words — plain English works best.

The three parts of a good prompt

Every good prompt has these parts:

  1. Who the AI is (optional) — “You are a helpful writing assistant.”
  2. What you want — “Write a short thank-you note.”
  3. Any important details — “It is for my nurse manager, for covering my shift last Tuesday.”

Copy-and-paste examples

Try these exactly as written. Replace the bracketed parts with your own words.

Example 1: Write an email

Write a short, polite email to [a coworker / a neighbor / a landlord]. The email should ask them to [do something specific]. Keep it under 100 words. Use a friendly tone.

Example 2: Explain something hard

Explain [topic] like I am 12 years old. Use short sentences and an example.

Example 3: Plan a meal

I have [ingredients you have]. Suggest a simple dinner I can make in under 30 minutes. List the steps.

Example 4: Summarize an article

Summarize this article in three short bullet points: [paste the article text, with names and addresses removed]

Tips for better answers

  • Be specific. “Write a letter” is vague. “Write a thank-you letter to my nurse manager” is specific.
  • Say how long. “In three sentences,” “Under 100 words,” or “One short paragraph.”
  • Say the tone. “Friendly,” “professional,” “simple,” or “humorous.”
  • Ask it to try again. If the first answer is not right, say “Try again, but make it shorter” or “Can you add more detail about X?”

What to do if the answer is wrong

AI can make things up. If an answer gives you a fact, a date, a law, or medical information, check it yourself before trusting it. AI is a starting point, not the final word.

Practice exercise

Open a free AI chat and type this:

Suggest three easy breakfast ideas that take under five minutes and use common grocery items.

Read the answer. Then ask a follow-up:

Make the second idea into a full recipe with steps.

That is a real conversation with AI. You just did it.
